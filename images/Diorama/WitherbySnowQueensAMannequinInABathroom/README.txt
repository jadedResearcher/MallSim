i threw this one together in a lunch break

the paintings are from jackElope, with the vague idea that since wibby only spawns in Lonely places, nothing
is more lonely than single person bathrooms with ads for sexy singles in them

i wanted something quick and easy so if the MEAT of the experiment failed, no real loss

i put spackle down in a small cardboard box (originally i intended to just do an ice test but i put too much spackle down and it was enough to do a whole room)

i carved tiles into the floor, not caring about realism, then super glued a toilet and a potted plant i had lying around

hardest part was making the wall art cuz i'd never done a frame before

then i had the mannequin i'd beheaded for camille's decapitated head so tossed it in so i could get a variety of surfaces to see how they react to the ice

i let the spackle dry, more or less, then put it in the freezer to get super cold, like you would an icecream maker container 

then i got a water sprayer, like for misting plants, that i had lying around, and every ten minutes (i work from home so i just got up during all my meetings occasionally) and took the box outta the freezer and misted it and put it back in.

i tried to keep it oriented with the floor down, so the ice would sit believably


once i was done with work i let it freeze a few hours while i did other things, then pulled it out and did camera and lightening tests with my phone, an endo scope and some fog in a can

its melting currently in my bathroom

big thing about this is that yeah its WAY faster and presumably more realistic than making fake resin ice

but it IS very temporary

thats fine tho

i like the idea of things being impermanent in these cases (i can't store infinite dioramas, lol)
